As part of the University of California, the California Digital Library (CDL)
is required to follow the
[University of California Information Technology Accessibility Policy](https://www.ucop.edu/electronic-accessibility/initiative/policy.html)
to help ensure that as broad a population as possible may access, benefit from,
and contribute to the University's electronic programs and services. The Policy
requires that all web content on arks.org complies with the
[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/)
at Level AA. Accordingly, contributors are responsible for ensuring that content
they provide to arks.org meets this standard.
